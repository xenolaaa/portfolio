Production & Guest Operations / Case Study

Dad’s Garage Theatre

Atlanta’s beloved improv theatre — and my longest-running classroom in guest operations. Live shows, real crowds, zero do-overs.

My Role
Front of House & Guest Operations
Where
Dad’s Garage Theatre, Atlanta
Tenure
Long-running — show night after show night
Format
Live improv & scripted productions

01 — Overview

Improv means nobody knows exactly what happens on stage. Front of house is where somebody absolutely has to.

Across years of show nights at Dad’s Garage, I’ve coordinated the volunteers and staff who turn a line on the sidewalk into a seated, happy, ready-to-laugh audience — arrival flow, seating, accessibility accommodations, and the patron-facing service that sets the tone before the first scene does.

02 — What I owned

The night, run smoothly

  • 1People coordination. Directing volunteers and staff on live show nights — who’s where, doing what, and what to do when the plan meets the public.
  • 2Arrival & seating flow. Queue to lobby to seat, tuned for speed without the cattle-call feeling.
  • 3Guest service & accessibility. Patron-facing problem-solving and accommodations, handled so the guest’s night never skips a beat.
  • 4Calm under fire. Sold-out houses, surprise situations, live-event chaos — resolved quietly, usually before anyone noticed there was something to resolve.

03 — Reflection

Years of live audiences taught me the operations instinct no classroom could: the guest experience is designed at the door, not just on the stage. It’s why I treat every queue, lobby, and seating chart like an interface — because to the guest, it is one.
